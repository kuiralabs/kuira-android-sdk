How the resize behaves

A small resize handle (the corner ⌟ ticks shown on the Card/Panel) tracks the drag and the widget snaps to the nearest tier on release — Peek ⇄ Pill ⇄ Card ⇄ Panel. Each tier is a distinct layout chosen by available size (BoxWithConstraints), not just a scaled version — so going bigger adds information (pools, dust, the runner sync track, quick actions) rather than stretching the pill. Going smaller sheds it back down to the single most important line. Tapping a Pill/Card/Panel still opens the full modal sheet; the tiers are the always-on, on-screen view.
