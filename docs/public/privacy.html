Privacy Policy

Effective: April 2026

Overview

Kuira Wallet ("the App") is a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet for the Midnight blockchain. Your private keys never leave your device. We do not operate servers that store your data.

Data We Collect

The App is designed to minimize data collection:

  • No account required. No email, phone number, or personal information is collected during wallet creation.
  • No analytics. We do not use analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.).
  • Crash reports. If you experience a crash, anonymized crash logs may be sent to Firebase Crashlytics. These contain: device model, OS version, stack trace, and a random installation ID. They do not contain wallet addresses, balances, transaction data, or any personal information.
  • Network requests. The App communicates directly with Midnight blockchain nodes and indexers to sync your balance and submit transactions. These are public blockchain endpoints — no personal data is sent beyond your public wallet address (which is already public on the blockchain).

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Wallet seed. Encrypted with a hardware-backed key in Android Keystore (StrongBox/TEE). The seed never leaves your device and cannot be extracted even with root access.
  • Wallet addresses. Cached locally for fast access.
  • Transaction cache. Recent transactions cached locally for offline viewing.
  • App preferences. Network selection, developer mode, proof server URL — stored in Android DataStore.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your recovery phrase (24 words)
  • Your private keys
  • Your wallet balance
  • Your transaction history
  • Your IP address
  • Your location
  • Any personal identifiers

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash reporting only. Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google) — feature flags (e.g., mainnet availability). No personal data transmitted.
  • Midnight blockchain nodes — public infrastructure, not operated by us.

Self-Custody Responsibility

Kuira Wallet is non-custodial. You are solely responsible for safeguarding your recovery phrase. If you lose your recovery phrase, your funds cannot be recovered by anyone — including us. We do not have access to your keys.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
